# Golden Thread birthday restoration

The birthday branch retains the original Golden Thread source. The party composes its original World, Sky, CharacterModel, wardrobe, Travellers controller, Guide, NPCs, Townsfolk, Animals, TownDressing, WonderSites, quests, economy, Housing/HousingView, Messages, caravan, VanInterior, Chariot, cake model and Festivities with invitation-filtered multiplayer. The original single-player entry and personal story/cinematics are not served.

The entire registered outfit collection is available: 127 base designs and 48 fusion designs, 175 total (96 girl and 79 boy designs). The dressing room includes cultural shelves, search, pagination, design notes, coverage details and one rotating model preview. The birthday guest can dress a connected private companion or observer; public guests cannot target or discover them. Outfits are validated and persisted by the party server.

The Great Palace is sourced from birthday-castle branch `claude/project-thread-ua4w3i`, commit `0ca8fb371203f5c3a1f7d2306add622fc6014b41`. Its complete architectural dependency closure lives in great-palace/. The palace stands beyond the existing northern lands; its elevated causeway crosses above them. The meadow lift, bridge pavilions, terrace gardens, Rose Gate, crown courtyard and four side venues form one connected route. Source collision and floor queries use the same transform as rendering. Shared coordinates drive the cake, race and courier objectives on both server and client.

Original progression is preserved: new profiles begin with 40 coins and walk, cape flight and Safar; there are no free unlock or finale flags. Crafts, vehicle prerequisites, friendships, animal care, crops and properties use the original rule modules. Movement reuses Travellers; its optional terrain adapter defaults to the original terrain functions. VanInterior retains its original appearance and default behavior, with its optional occupant flag disabled in the party so it never fabricates a private companion. Local findings, quests, inventory and property are saved per invitation in this browser. Party movement, chosen outfits, current rides, rounds, Twenty Lights progress and album remain server-backed.

The Company panel restores the original fictional travelling children and pets, original chapter and befriending prerequisites, food-gated pet feeding, conversation and sending companions home. These local NPCs do not use or reveal private player positions.

Story connects four parts of one game: the original twenty-land chapters and side quests; The Twenty Lights cooperative birthday trail; nine shared minigames; and the Great Palace evening. The birthday trail and completed game rounds contribute public-safe keepsakes to the finale journal. The host can gather everyone whenever ready; finishing all quests or games is optional. The finale advances through wish lanterns, birthday-only candle blowing and cake cutting at the actual cake, fireworks, then the afterparty. The cake uses the original rose/bead/ribbon geometry and separates its first slice. The original Festivities supplies petals, rainbow dust, lanterns, auroras, unicorns, a circling dragon and NPC guests. Completion earns the original light and dragon unlock once.

AbdulAzim remains a private observer who follows Fathima. Private companions and their vehicle visuals/thread are excluded from the public polaroid render, presence, scores and voice projection. Existing invitation URLs are unchanged. No personal invitation config or original story entry is emitted by the build.

## Verification

25 authoritative party tests, 10 chase tests, 4 cooperative story tests, 5 festival tests, 3 voice privacy tests and 6 hosted Worker scenarios pass. Ten offline regression groups exercise original economy/skill gates, locally solvable chapters, the full meadow quest, finale and vehicle prerequisites, housing/farms/animals, messages/gifts, save/load, all 175 modest outfits, long randomized follow spacing and caravan rules. These do not replace the original Vitest suite.

A CPU scene check constructs the actual Great Palace and Golden Thread world, samples the eight bridge segments, checks all nine destinations and five race gates, the two lift endpoints, and all forty story stations. It checks that unrelated Golden Thread terrain remains unchanged. Extended checks exercise populated towns, original defaults, caravan management, follow spacing, cape energy drain, rides, original festivities, candle/slice/fireworks stages and idempotent finale rewards.

GPU/browser rendering, real microphone calls and fourteen graphical clients remain unverified: local browser/server execution was blocked by the session sandbox. The original Vitest/typecheck dependencies were unavailable; the attempt to install them was interrupted while awaiting network permissions. No full-suite pass is claimed. Automatic graphics reduces render cost on sustained slow frames; high/low settings remain available.
