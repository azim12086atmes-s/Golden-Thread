# Original game parity notes

The party wardrobe uses the original character outfit registry and its dressing-room metadata. The registry contains 127 researched and extended outfits, plus 48 deterministic fusion designs: 175 total, including 96 girl-design and 79 boy-design entries. The fusion set contributes 24 designs per gender. Wardrobe shelves are All, East Asia, South & SE Asia, Middle East & Africa, Europe & Americas, Fantasy & Sci-fi, and Fusion.

The party dressing room provides a culture shelf filter, a gender filter, text search, paginated outfit swatches, colour and culture metadata, the source design note and coverage layers, and one rotating CharacterModel preview. The preview keeps the observer's original boy identity even when a different-gender design is previewed. The birthday guest can select a connected private companion or observer as the target; only the birthday view includes those role-tagged private target options. Observers remain view-only.

The birthday client runs the original twenty-land World with streaming region lifecycle callbacks, Sky and atmospheric effects, CharacterModel and Thread, and the Travellers movement/flight/energy rules. Original NPCs, townsfolk, animals, dressing and wonders populate streamed lands. The original quest, economy, friendship/message, farming, animal care, housing and vehicle prerequisites run through their original modules; the birthday panels expose those actions in the shared interface. The original fictional caravan has its own Company panel and follows only the local avatar with original spacing and membership rules. Safar retains its original cabin and decorations.

Story links the original journal, the shared Twenty Lights trail, nine minigames and the Great Palace celebration. The party uses the original Chariot, pure CakeModel and Festivities; the original single-player CakeScene imports the extracted model without changes to its choreography. The shared ceremony substitutes birthday wishes, candle blowing, cake cutting and an afterparty for private relationship captions. Its completion earns the original celebration light and dragon unlock once. Public build output excludes the original story/cinematic files and the standalone original game entry, as required for this birthday.

Gameplay progression is saved locally per invitation; party movement, outfits, rides, rounds, shared birthday story and album use the server. The original future expansion requirements are not claimed as completed features. Verification and browser/device limitations are recorded in BIRTHDAY-RESTORATION.md.
