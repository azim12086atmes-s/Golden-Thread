# Golden Thread spatial and ground audit

Source of truth reviewed: `azim12086atmes-s/Golden-Thread`, branch `claudes-current-work`, pinned commit `a6c20a4f9ceabec4fbd544ca7c73658b005c2318` (the published `gh-pages` reference identified by the parent). The local `/workspace/golden-thread` tree is stale (`latest-working-on-going` snapshot at `acbaee3`): its `src/world/World.ts` does not import the active Meadow, wind, foliage, water, or pattern-ground systems described here. Conclusions below are for the pinned reference, not that checkout.

## Active scene path and spatial contracts

The canonical app entrypoint remains `index.html` -> `src/main.ts` -> `Game`; in this pinned revision `Game` constructs `World` and calls `World.update(focus, night)` each frame. `World` imports `MeadowField` and `patternGround` from `src/world/Meadow.ts`, `swayMaterial` from `wind.ts`, foliage materials/cards from `foliage.ts`, and flowing/built water from `Water.ts`/`flowWater.ts`. This directly disproves the prior local-snapshot audit's claim that the rich meadow is unconnected.

The shared spatial units are metres, x/z horizontal, y elevation, with each region centered on the regular grid (`regionCenter`) and terrain sampled from `terrainHeight(x,z)`. Terrain is the canonical ground source for rendering, actors, and decoration. It blends region relief/ground palettes, adds seeded landforms and dune/snow/rock shaping, carves named water bodies with sloped banks, levels plots and the celebration castle site, and exposes `groundAt`/`surfaceAt` plus raised `Platform`s for walkable non-terrain surfaces. Terrain renders as 160 m chunks with a 40-by-40 vertex grid and vertex colors. `World` streams chunks around the player and calls `meadow.update(focus, terrainHeight(...))`.

Town placement is a spatializer, not independent random props. `RegionBuilder` builds deterministic per-region town instances, with avenue/ring-road constraints, reserved landmark footprints, paved footprints and plot footprints; building colliders/platforms follow those placements. `townLayout.ts` provides lane and lot generation for grid towns. `roads.ts` drapes strips/discs against the ground-height function. `paved.ts` stores spatially indexed paved shapes and dirty areas; Meadow invalidates/replants affected cells when paving changes. `reserved.ts` declares exclusion bounds/landmark grounds. `neighbourhood.ts` supplies civic building and market layouts. `landforms.ts` supplies seeded region-specific mesa/butte/peak/arctic/karst/granite/volcano/hill geometry and matching height/color fields; `waters.ts` carries river/lake bodies and carve logic. `World` additionally owns landmark and region collider registration, streamed region load/unload, water shores/current meshes, and view-distance culling. Decoration samples those same spatial APIs, so props avoid water, roads, paving, plots, buildings, and reserved clearings rather than overlaying them arbitrarily.

## Grass, ground, wind, and palette

`MeadowField` is active and is a seven-mesh instanced system: close grass, close flowers, mid/far grass, mid flowers, sky-island crystals, and sand pebbles. Crossed picture-card tufts and flower cards use generated canvas textures, per-instance tint/scale/orientation, and shared gust-driven vertex displacement. Near grass spacing is 0.34 m over a 190-cell grid (~65 m), flowers 0.7 m over 92 cells (~64 m); middle grass is 1 m out to 100 m, far grass is 2.6 m out to 240 m. Instances recycle on a fixed world grid around the focus, so moving the view does not reroll the landscape. The group turns off when the player is 60 m above ground; low graphics can disable the field and a struggling-device adaptation removes the far ring.

`grassy`/`openGround` use terrain color and height plus exclusions: only green ground above water; no central plaza/landmark, avenue and pavement margins, ring road, region highways, paved footprints, or plots. Town grass is probabilistically thinned. `sandy` areas receive pebbles; Sky Isles receive crystal clusters. Instance placement uses the same `terrainHeight` and deterministic cell hashes. `patternGround` also actively modifies the terrain material: world-coordinate multi-scale patches, mown bands, sand grain/ripples, snow drift/cracks/tracks, green blossom flecks, dusk fairy rings and lamp pools. These are shader details layered on physical terrain, not flat image cards.

One shared `wind.ts` defines land-specific wind strength and common direction/time/gust uniforms. The same `WIND_GLSL` drives grass, tree crowns/cards, foliage, flags/petals and related materials; leaves additionally use sun/night/season tint and lamp uniforms. Terrain patterns align to the wind direction for sand and snow. Grass palette starts from region `ground`/`groundAlt` vertex colors and adds per-cell green hue/saturation/lightness variation; flowers use the current land's configured flower and night-light palettes. Surface-aware `swayMaterial` resolves per-color material indices and fades the detailed surface treatment with distance. Tree crowns are blob meshes plus atlas leaf cards, rather than simple spheres alone.

## Review findings / limits

- Read files and public exports at the pinned revision; no files were edited except this audit report.
- Local source must be refreshed from the pinned revision before implementation decisions based on this report. In particular local `src/world/World.ts`, `terrain.ts`, and lack of `Meadow.ts` are not authoritative for this build.
- The last confirmed commit message reports a measured scene cost around 4.3 million triangles/frame, including ~0.9 million from nearby monuments and ~1 million from grass. Its adaptation culls monuments beyond haze and drops far grass in lighter/struggling mode. This is useful performance context, but not a substitute for GPU profiling on target devices.
- Some tree/terrain/water modules were inspected by source and active import graph, not run in a browser; no visual or GPU claim is made here.
