## Observed

Golden Thread's dense, multicolored flowers and grass, populated Great Oak, treehouse, cultural clothing, glowing thread, original chariot, rainbow and world props are present in the actual pinned game. Its coordinated visual systems should be preserved.

The selected Birthday Castle source shows the huge palace, modeled mountain enclosure, high plateau, stepped terraces and human-scale roof court. Daylight and night views show broad garden surfaces that read as repeated flat patterns, alongside authored tree/flower/fountain props. Those surfaces need a deliberate visual improvement consistent with Golden Thread; importing the full donor alone does not turn them into organic gardens.

The corrected camera inside the floating meadow shows dense authored grass blades, flowers, shrubs and trees. The earlier camera was just outside the 80 m island boundary and was not a valid grass review. Both repositories have active grass; camera/height/clearance contracts matter.

The lower approach diagnostic was taken at y22 and looks through lower route vegetation/structure. It does not frame the newer 8 km SkyBridge at y688, or validate its deck/collisions. Source layout also retains an older near-water causeway. Keep rendered bridge geometry, dynamic viaduct data, registered walkable pads and entry routes coherent.
