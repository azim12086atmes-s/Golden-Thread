## Palace and terrain comparison

There are two materially different palace implementations, not one branch that simply has a larger file. The playable family (`play`, `game-logic`, `world-slice`, and `joyful-minigames`) contains the 147,751-byte `js/world/palace.js` at blob SHA `70c3fa4f3ed24956c4d747402c85d6df91df2656`, a large authored procedural palace module at 2.75× scale and a broad spatial plan. Crucially, this is already in a complete playable game tree: `game-logic` and `play` have an identical `js/main.js` blob and `layout.js` blob; `joyful-minigames` uses the exact same palace blob and a later/different layout. These branches import and instantiate the palace, terrain, meadow, nature, approach, multiplayer and gameplay systems. `project-thread-ua4w3i` has a similarly sized but **different and earlier** palace blob (`a42d74e02fed026aa092cbe759302947e07b0b37`) and different layout; its tree is only a partial source slice. It is not the only home of the large palace.

For the selected playable donor, `game-logic`/`play`, `S=2.75` and `PAL.y=PAL1.y*S=290*2.75=797.5` m (the x1 podium datum). The plan's x bounds are `-385*S..450*S` (2,296.25 m wide); the main tier plan runs from z `-338*S` to `235*S`, with front corner towers reaching z about `262*S`. The Rose Gate face is z `205*S=563.75` m and its sill is `PAL.y+52*S=940.5` m above sea level. Walkable roof tiers are computed as `PAL.y + top*S`: 940.5, 1,221, 1,309, and 1,391.5 m. The causeway is z `1060*S..2262*S` (2,915..6,220.5 m), at a base y of 6 m before it rises to the B20 plaza; B20 is `(0,13.75,6421.25)`. The separate cloud-platform spawn is `(-710,12020)` in x/z; its y is computed by `groundAt` in `main.js`, so the source constant alone does not specify the spawn elevation. The selected palace source raises spire tops to 1.6 times their roof-relative height (and dome towers/cone to 1.35); its tallest listed needle reaches about 2,175 m absolute when applying the documented layout scale. These values come from the pinned game-logic `layout.js` and `palace.js`, not the different `ua4w3i` prototype. `joyful-minigames` preserves the exact palace blob and same 2.75× scale, but its 32 KB layout has changed from the 37.7 KB `game-logic`/`play` layout (including the causeway start and spawn arrangement); do not pair a palace blob with another branch's spatial layout without checking the full relationship.

The best integrated reference is the identical reviewed world-code family at `main` / `claude/palace-meshy-kit-eh4g5c` / `claude/project-thread-0gs0gp` / `claude/project-thread-iptprt` / `design/image-library-2026-10-13`. The entrypoint actively builds a ~300 m-class castle on the central crag, with separate `castle.js` (~33 KB), `castleArch.js` (~18 KB), and `castleDetail.js` (~8 KB), plus the complete approach, forest, districts, acropolis, garden, monuments, islands, nature, meadow, atmosphere, and sky modules. Coordinates in this family are explicitly metres and north is negative z: crag centered `(0,5)`, radius `(250,215)`, height 90 m; gate plane `(0,30)`, sill/elevation 180 m; terrace walk levels 120/150/180 m; arrival `(0,942)` at y34; grand approach follows a 9 m half-width axis from z935 to z230, then terrain/approach geometry covers toward ground at z790. This is a complete world-layout/terrain/collider closure, rather than the large isolated palace file.

Both families have organic ground systems, but they are different revisions. The integrated family actively constructs `createMeadow` and `createNature` from `js/main.js`; meadow plants camera-following instanced tufts and flowers on `layout.groundAt`, skipping built footprint and non-natural terrain through `builtHeight`, `terrainHeight`, and `isClearOfBuildings`. Nature independently scatters instanced tree crowns/cards, bushes and flowers over the land/islands. `layout.js` is the spatial authority for `groundAt`, `terrainHeight`, `builtHeight`, `resolveCollisions`, `isClearOfBuildings`, island elevations and portal/road lines. `approach.js` follows `AXIS_LINE` from that same layout and updates its animation each frame. Materials provide toon/texture/night grading and animated meadow/wind shading. In the older `project-thread-hnjcxb`/`game-logic` family, `ground.js` tessellates the baked terrain with coarse and near fine-relief grids; the playable heads report quality-dependent 3–6 m near cells, while `meadow.js` uses generated near/mid instanced grass and terrain occupancy textures.

## Recommendation and provenance limits

For a complete game that already includes the physically huge palace, use the playable big-palace family rather than substituting the smaller current `main` castle. The safest donor is `claude/game-logic-4d13do` at `3fcc2b4728c51eca871c889fa545c9605d7ad8df`; `claude/world-slice-qsc3ua` is an exact duplicate head. Relative to `play` at `433ea333a877270d172a49ea142ab5af47f83622`, the reviewed runtime source files (`*.js`, `*.html`, `*.css`) have identical hashes, including `js/main.js`, `js/world/palace.js`, `js/world/layout.js`, and the old ground/meadow/sky/tree stack. The game-logic recursive tree is fuller (2,433 vs 1,157 entries), so it keeps the matching playable source with a fuller asset/docs set. The `joyful-minigames-pets-ung50s` branch is also a merge from game-logic and retains the identical large-palace blob, but its `main.js`, coast, ground, layout, meadow, sky, and trees differ and it omits brook; the layout is a different spatial revision. No distinct pet feature API was evident from the module/import comparison, so the branch name is not enough reason to prefer that tree. Its minigame, celebration, voice, and network modules are present in game-logic too. The current `main`/`palace-meshy-kit` family is the separately integrated, smaller-castle architecture; it remains useful as a source of modular arch/detail treatment, but it is not the replacement for the requested largest palace. `/workspace/palace-candidate` is still only the incomplete `ua4w3i` slice.

Feature/import evidence in game-logic `js/main.js` includes `Avatar`/`OUTFITS`, `createStardustHunt`/`createWishLanterns`, `createCelebration`/`playWelcome`, `Net`, `Voice`, and the travel, lobby, clock, places, ships, combat, mew, quest, and progress modules. These are source-closure findings, not assertions that each path was browser-run.

The audit used GitHub branch heads, recursive tree listings, pinned raw source files and commit metadata, plus local file-list comparison against `/workspace/palace-candidate`. No game files were edited, and no visual/runtime claims are made from these source checks.
