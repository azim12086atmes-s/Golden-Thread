# Golden Thread visual audit (pinned source)

Reference: `azim12086atmes-s/Golden-Thread` commit `a6c20a4f9ceabec4fbd544ca7c73658b005c2318` (`claudes-current-work`). Comparison target: party source in `/workspace/golden-thread` at audit time. This corrects the earlier audit, which described the older `latest-working-on-going` revision.

## Visual grammar and scale

- `src/characters/CharacterModel.ts` explicitly defines soft rounded forms, a floating faceless head, full-coverage clothing and tagged parts. Outfit construction uses cloth materials from `characters/fabric.ts` (deterministic painted motifs/grain and separate motif glow), flowing hems, and `characters/wings.ts` stained-glass swallowtail wings. The wing pair is intentionally enormous: `HER_HEIGHT=1.78`, `WING_H=7.5*HER_HEIGHT`, with swept-back span, colored glass shader, black/gold leading, veins, glints and beat animation. `Travellers.ts` enforces wing clearance/folding and companion positioning. This is a core visual, not an optional accessory.
- Vehicle materials use physically based finishes, a RoomEnvironment reflection map, and fade those reflections at night (`vehicles.ts`, Game constructor). Rounded forms, warm detailed finishes, selective bloom, and subtle fabric/material emission are more representative than flat saturated shapes or uniformly glowing surfaces.
- Two-hero scale and contact constraints persist: `CharacterModel` uses identity-specific hero scale; `Travellers.ts` maintains separate seats/mounts, positions the boy under/beside or in front of wings as appropriate, folds wings when riding/nearby, and resolves collision before final gap checks. `Thread.ts` is a fine gold core in a narrow warm halo with motes; it should read as a wrist-to-wrist tether, not a thick neon cable.

## Current-source scene systems

- `World.ts` streams real regional terrain, landmarks, water, and towns, and owns `MeadowField`. It imports `Meadow.ts` patterned ground, locale-tinted flowers/grass, and the foliage/water/wind systems. Meadow is a dense set of crossed, textured grass tufts, colored by region and moved by wind; patterned ground includes mown bands, blossom speckles and night fairy rings. Green grass is constrained away from sand, snow, roads and plaza, and thins in towns. Tree/leaf materials sway; rivers have foam and flowing highlights.
- `Sky.ts` is procedural rather than a flat sky color: shader dome with sun scattering and moving thin-cloud veils, stars and moon, plus locale tints. `SkyFX` adds region/time-specific sky ornaments and celebration effects. `Weather` contributes regional dust/snow/mist/haze/pollen, while `Ambience` and `RegionFX` add localized particles and grading.
- Game constructor also installs `Atmos`, `Traffic`, `FestivalAir`, `CityScreens`, `CivicMarkers`, `Dragons`, plus World/Sky/ambience/lanterns/SkyFX/weather. `Traffic` and `Weather` are explicit properties in Game; World hooks load/unload NPCs, townsfolk, dressing, animals and housing as regions stream. The frame loop updates the corresponding systems. Beyond the older snapshot's list, current world presentation includes moving road/water/air traffic, regional weather, festival aircraft, city screens and civic markers. It is incorrect to report these as dormant in the pinned source.
- Composer remains Render → Bloom → RegionFX grade → Output. Game uses ACES, shadow mapping with reduced redraw work, `RoomEnvironment` for vehicles, and adaptive resolution/grass quality; after sustained slow frames it drops quality. `PartyAtmosphere` in the old party source restores Ambience/RegionFX/SkyFX/SkyLanterns and a bloom/grade pipeline but uses a simpler bloom setup and its own quality policy.

## Concrete party gaps in the compared source

- Correction after inspecting the later party source: `party/life.js` (not `PartyGame`'s constructor in `main.js`) creates original Npcs, Townsfolk, Animals and TownDressing, installs `World.onRegionLoaded`/`onRegionUnloaded`, processes already loaded regions, and updates those systems. They are active in the party. The compared party still does not set up the original `Atmos`, Weather, Traffic, FestivalAir, CityScreens, CivicMarkers, or Dragons systems; those remain concrete gaps relative to pinned `Game.ts`.
- Party scenery adds invitation/castle destinations through `PartyScenery` and `Adventures` alongside the older streamed Golden Thread `World`. The old party source's `World` has no `MeadowField`; the experimental local `party/living-grass.js` is a separate, unpublished implementation. It does not restore the newer authored Meadow, wind, lamp and terrain shader contract. When migrating to the pinned current `World`, remove or deliberately scope that experimental grass to avoid duplicate planting.
- Party keeps original CharacterModel/Travellers/vehicle code, but its visible player models are separate server-filtered models; the controller's own pair lives in an offscreen scene. `party/main.js` only assigns girl/boy identity scaling to birthday/observer roles, leaves other guest models neutral, and separately draws the birthday-observer thread. That is a role/privacy behavior, while it also means the original persistent paired-character presentation is not globally shown to guests. Name sprites and status rings are party UI additions.
- Camera comparison: original Game camera is FOV 60; original `Travellers` starts at distance 8, pitch .32 and adapts vehicle framing. Party uses FOV 57 and initial distance 10, with a separately smoothed player camera. Large wings are >13m tall before character scale and need an explicit camera/framing check in flight and on mounts; the old party camera defaults are much tighter than the wing extents suggest.
