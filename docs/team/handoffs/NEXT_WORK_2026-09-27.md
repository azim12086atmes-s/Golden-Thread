## C. Every intent, interpreted into buildable specifications

Group numbers are for reference; see section D for the order.

### C1. Charity and sponsorship (the heart of this batch)

The owner wants the game's economy to be about *doing good*: sponsoring and caring for people, funded by the player's
work, paid for not only in coins but in food, rations, necessities and amenities.

- **People who can be sponsored**: orphans, elderly people, homeless people, poor families. Generate them per land as
  townsfolk (`npc/Townsfolk.ts`, `npc/folk.ts`) with a need (food, shelter, schooling, medicine, work).
- **Sponsoring** = a recurring commitment (daily/weekly in game time) of coins *or goods* (meals, rations, blankets,
  medicine, books, school fees). Track in `GameState` (new field, e.g. `sponsorships: Array<{ personId, land, needs,
  since, paidUntil }>`, added in `newGame()` so `deserialize` fills old saves). Sponsored people improve visibly
  (happier, better clothes, attend school, find work) and write to the player (`social/` messages).
- **"Sponsoring and granting them in your own home"** / **"Build new floors in homes to accommodate and sponsor more
  people"**: the player's own home (`housing/`, `state.plots`, `state.homes`, the interior in `HouseInterior.ts` with
  `furnishHome`) gains **capacity**: each floor adds rooms/beds; sponsored people can live there. Needs: a "build a
  floor" action on an owned home (cost + build time), the exterior house (`buildDecor('house-<land>')`) growing a storey
  per floor, and the interior showing the extra rooms/residents. Residents are townsfolk figures placed in the home.
- **Soup kitchens**: a building/institution where meals are cooked and given out; the player supplies ingredients
  (farm produce, bought rations) and can **employ a chef** (C3) to cook for others. Show queues of people being fed.
- **Charity employment** (2:08 pm): employ people in charitable service (e.g. a chef cooking for the poor) and pay them
  **in housing, food, necessities and the opportunities they want** (training, schooling, a path to their own trade),
  not only coins.

### C2. Institutions you can build and upgrade

- **Healthcare**: clinics **upgradable to hospitals** (tiers: clinic → health centre → hospital), staffed by doctors and
  nurses you hire; patients arrive; treatment uses medicine items; each land's own medicine (see C5).
- **Libraries and knowledge institutions**: libraries (the earlier brief also asks for physical libraries — see
  `REQUIREMENTS_EXPANSION.md`), archives, observatories.
- **Education**: schools, **madrasas**, **tent schools** (for the desert/Bedouin land and wherever people are poor),
  **universities**, and **"Hogwarts"** — a magical school/university in the Meadow's fairy-tale style (castle towers,
  great hall, floating candles/lanterns; peaceful, no combat).
- **Every place has its own science** — "search what every place has" (do real research, cite sources in a new
  `docs/research/` file): e.g. China — traditional Chinese medicine, silk, porcelain, printing; Japan — joinery,
  metallurgy/sword-smithing as craft (not weapons use), robotics; Korea — hangul/printing, celadon, ondol heating;
  Islamic lands — astronomy, optics, algebra, hospitals (bimaristan), libraries (House of Wisdom); Middle East — navigation,
  desalination, falconry; Egypt — surveying, papyrus, irrigation (nilometer); desert — water finding, star navigation;
  North India — Ayurveda, astronomy (Jantar Mantar), textiles/dyeing; South India — Ayurveda/Siddha, metallurgy (wootz),
  spice agronomy, temple engineering; Mughal — garden hydraulics, miniature painting, architecture; Indonesia — rice
  terrace irrigation (subak), batik, boat building; Norway — shipbuilding, fisheries, hydropower; Switzerland —
  watchmaking, precision engineering, pharmacy; London — engineering, clockmaking, the Royal Institution; New Yonder —
  computing, renewable energy, biotech; Renaissance — anatomy/art, optics, architecture, banking; Vintage town —
  radio/early electronics; Aurora — polar science, reindeer herding; Sky Isles — "magic science"/light; Meadow — magic.
  Each land gets **institute buildings for its science** in its own architectural style (`traditions.ts`/`facade.ts`).
- **Build and upgrade**: institutions are placed on owned land (`housing/` plots, build mode `buildPanel` in `UI.ts`),
  take **time to build**, and can be upgraded in tiers.

### C3. Hiring and employing people (employees, freelancers)

- **Hire people to**: teach, cook, build houses, **lay tents**, **weave fabric**, deliver goods, treat patients, research.
- Two modes: **employ** (ongoing wage, housing, food) or **pay as a freelancer** (one job, one payment).
- **Payment in kind**: coins, food, rations, necessities, amenities, housing, or opportunities (training, a course).
- **Delivery workers** ("hiring people to deliver goods" + "supply chain logic"): goods move between lands/markets on
  vehicles along the roads — reuse `src/traffic/` designs (vans, trucks, carts, camel caravans, boats, drones) as the
  visible carriers; a hired courier follows a route and delivers to a market/stall/institution; supply and demand per
  land (`economy/`) sets prices.
- Builders: constructing a home or institution "takes time"; hiring a builder speeds it up; paying partly in food/rations.

### C4. Skills, services, study and research

- **Selling a service requires a skill level; to grow a skill you have to do it** (practice-based progression). Skills
  live in `state.skills` (weaving, carpentry, cooking, pottery, calligraphy, gardening, mechanics, lampcraft) — extend
  with teaching, medicine, research, building, software, hardware, logistics, etc. Using a skill raises it; services
  unlock at thresholds and pay more at higher levels.
- **Teaching as a service**: the player (or hired teachers) teach at schools/madrasas/universities; earns coins/goods and
  raises the teaching skill.
- **Interning, doing a course, working under professors**: enrol in a course at an institution (time + fee, paid in coins
  or goods), attend lessons, get a certificate that unlocks a skill tier or a job.
- **Research and a thesis from professors**: take a research position under a professor; produce a **thesis** —
  **scientific thesis** (per the land's science, C2) or **magic thesis** (Meadow/Sky Isles "Hogwarts") — as a multi-step
  project; completing it grants a degree, reputation, an invention or a recipe.
- **Service jobs** from the earlier list: company employee, **freelance software**, **hardware building** (New Yonder /
  London / Switzerland tech firms), plus health care, tech, logistics, supply, agriculture, research, manufacturing,
  inventions.

### C5. Agriculture and supply

- **Agriculture land**: larger fields than the current farm beds (`housing/housing.ts` `CROPS`, `farmbed`), per-land
  crops, hire farm workers, harvest and **grow and sell** at markets; supplies soup kitchens and institutions.
- **Supply chain logic**: producers → couriers → markets/institutions; stock levels; each land's specialities and
  wants (`regions.ts` `materials`/`wanted` already exist) drive trade.

### C6. World and city dressing

- **Bridges between water bodies**: rivers/lakes (`world/waters.ts`) currently have no bridges (water is walkable).
  Add bridges where roads/paths cross rivers, in each land's style (stone arch, wooden, Chinese moon bridge, Japanese
  arched red bridge, suspension, etc.).
- **Water lotus ponds**: more lotus ponds (`Water.ts` `lotusSpots` exists) — dedicated lotus ponds in India, Mughal
  gardens, China, Japan, Indonesia; glowing at night.
- **City lighting** — every land its own:
  - **London: street lighting** (Victorian gas-lamp posts along every street and the ring road).
  - **Tents (desert, Aurora lavvu): lamps** hung at tent entrances and inside.
  - **Gulabi Nagar ("gulbargha"): diyas** — rows of small clay oil lamps on steps, parapets, windowsills and jharokhas.
  - **Madinat an-Nur: hanging lantern lamps** — pierced brass lanterns hung in streets, arches and courtyards.
  - **Chinese sky lanterns** — many kongming lanterns rising over Jade Terraces (more than now; `SkyLanterns.ts`).
  - Existing: `world/lanterns.ts` (per-land lantern designs), `lampPost` in `architecture.ts`, `TownDressing.ts`.
- **New Yonder**: **TV panels** (big glowing screens/billboards on towers — animated content, peaceful: art, nature,
  news tickers), **more cyberpunk and solarpunk artifacts** (neon signage, holograms, vertical farms, solar trees,
  wind walls, monorails, drones docks, green bridges), and **penthouses** (luxury rooftop homes with terraces, pools,
  gardens — ideally buyable/enterable like other homes).
- **Balloons**: as **house decor** (buildable decor item in build mode and home interior options), **children holding
  balloons** (a balloon on a string above a child's hand; children are in `caravan/` and townsfolk), and **in the skies**
  (more balloon clusters; `SkyOrnaments` has hot-air balloons already).

### C7. Owning institutes: build them in stages, or grow inside established ones (owner, later on 27 Sep)

Verbatim:

> Being able to own these institute buildings by building them, we need to create in already established institutes,
> when we build we build in stages example swiss watch making begins by making a boutique, having a skill or know
> someone who we can pay or teach someone we have sponsored or sponsor someone who wants to learn

Specification:

- **Two routes to an institute.** (a) **Found and own one**: build it yourself on your own land. (b) **Work within an
  already established institute**: each land's towns already have institutes for their science (C2); you can join,
  study, intern, teach or research there (C4) before or instead of founding your own.
- **Build in stages, each a real, usable building**, growing in size and function. The owner's example, Swiss
  watchmaking: **a boutique** (a small shop selling and repairing watches) → a workshop/atelier → a manufacture
  (factory) → a watchmaking school/institute. Give every land's science its own ladder in its own architecture, e.g.:
  Chinese medicine: herb stall → apothecary → clinic → TCM hospital/academy; Ayurveda (India): herb garden → vaidya's
  dispensary → Ayurvedic clinic → college; Kerala spices: spice stall → plantation → trading house; Islamic sciences:
  bookseller → library → madrasa → university/observatory; London engineering: clock repair shop → workshop → works →
  institution; New Yonder tech: garage start-up → studio → company tower → research campus; Meadow magic: wand/potion
  shop → guild hall → "Hogwarts"-style school. Each stage has a cost, a build time, and unlocks services and jobs.
- **Every stage needs someone who has the skill.** Three ways to supply it, all allowed:
  1. **The player has the skill** (skills grow only by practising them — C4).
  2. **Know someone and pay them**: hire a skilled person (employee or freelancer — C3), paid in coins or in kind.
  3. **Teach someone you sponsor**, or **sponsor someone who wants to learn** — a sponsored person (C1) can be trained by
     the player or enrolled in a course, and then staffs the institute. This ties sponsorship, teaching and ownership
     into one loop: sponsor → teach/train → they work in and grow the institute → it serves others (charity, C1).
- State: an institute record per owned site `{ id, land, science, stage, builtAt, staff: personId[], skillNeeded }`;
  pure rules for `canAdvance(stage)` checking cost (coins or goods), build time and a qualified staff member.
