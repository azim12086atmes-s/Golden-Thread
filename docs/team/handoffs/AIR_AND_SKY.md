# What belongs in each land's air (audit, 2026-09-28)

What is already in each sky (world/skies.ts ornaments + traffic/roster.ts sky traffic), and what is
added now (✚). "Air decorations" are also the things hung *across* the streets — strings of
lanterns, flags and garlands between the houses — which every land decorates in its own way.
