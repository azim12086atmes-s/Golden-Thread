# Buildings & Artifacts — visual reference per land

Research pass: 2026-09-25. These notes feed `src/world/kit.ts` and the land palettes in
`src/world/regions.ts`. All descriptions are our own words; nothing was copied, no images were saved.
Everything here is to be rebuilt as original procedural low-poly geometry.

**How to read each land**
(a) signature buildings, one line each (silhouette · materials · colours) ·
(b) hero landmark · (c) man-made props · (d) natural artifacts & sky ·
(e) palette (5 hex, saturated, mid-to-high value per ART_BIBLE §4) · (f) sources.

**Cross-cutting rules that came out of the research**

- **No faces anywhere, including on buildings.** Several traditions put faces on structures:
  Korean jangseung posts, Egyptian sphinxes and colossi, Balinese guardian statues, kirtimukha
  masks on gopurams, gargoyles. Build these as faceless forms — a carved post with bands, a
  lion-bodied plinth with a smooth dome head, an abstract tiered ornament — or leave them out.
- **Temples and shrines as abstract silhouettes, never as idols.** Gopuram, vimana, meru, stupa and
  pagoda tiers carry their identity in the *stepped silhouette and colour*, not in figural sculpture.
  Replace sculpted figures with repeated geometric niches, lotus bands and finials.
- **Cultural care for the Arctic land.** It is *inspired by* Sámi and Finnish Lapland, not a copy:
  use the lavvu / goahti silhouettes and reindeer herding, but do not reproduce the sacred drum or
  exact gákti (costume) band patterns; invent a band pattern in the same spirit.
- **Light props are the bloom budget.** Every land below lists at least three light-carrying props
  (lantern, lamp, window, neon). Those are the only things that should cross the bloom threshold.

## 1. Wanderers' Meadow — high grassland cottages, rainbows, stars

**(a) Signature buildings**
1. *Round flower cottage* — squat drum walls of cream cob, conical thatch roof painted with flower
   rosettes, one round window, a stubby chimney.
2. *Shepherd's hut on wheels* — rounded corrugated roof, plank body in sage green, iron wheels, a
   three-step ladder; the natural cousin of the van *Safar*.
3. *Post windmill* — tapered timber body on a stone base, four lattice sails in white/red.
4. *Stone byre with turf roof* — low dry-stone walls, grass growing on the roof ridge.
5. *Beehive shelter (bee bole wall)* — stone wall with niches holding straw skeps.
6. *Dovecote* — tall round white tower with a tiny cone roof and rows of perch holes.
7. *Wayside bakery* — thatch over a bread oven dome, bench outside.
8. *Star-watching tower* — slender wooden lookout with a small domed brass telescope cap.

**(b) Hero landmark** — **The Great Oak & Wishing Well**: a huge rounded oak canopy (low-poly
clumped spheres) with a rope-and-bucket well under it; lanterns hang from the lower branches, and
Grandmother Sarvatara's cottage sits beside it.

**(c) Man-made props** — straw skeps on stands · rope-and-bucket wishing well · dry-stone walls
with stile steps · wooden five-bar gates · hay bales (round) · milk churns · wooden signposts with
arrow boards · clothes lines with bright quilts · picnic blanket & basket · wheelbarrow of flowers ·
hanging jar lanterns (firefly jars) · weathervane · bird boxes · stepping-stone path · bunting.

**(d) Natural artifacts** — rolling flower fields (poppy red, cornflower blue, buttercup yellow,
lavender) · lone standing stones · a gentle brook with a plank bridge · round-canopy oaks and
hawthorn · dandelion clocks drifting · **rainbow arcs by day** (double after rain) · meteor streaks,
dense star field and **fireflies** at night · low cumulus "sheep clouds".

**(e) Palette** — `#8FD16A` meadow green · `#F6E3B4` cob cream · `#E86F5A` poppy/roof red ·
`#7FB8F0` sky blue · `#FFD95A` buttercup glow

## 2. Japan

**(a) Signature buildings**
1. *Machiya townhouse* — narrow two-storey front, dark timber, fine vertical **kōshi / senbon-goshi**
   slat lattice, deep eaves, a **noren** curtain in the door.
2. *Minka farmhouse* — massive steep thatch (irimoya hip-and-gable), dark beams, raised **engawa**
   veranda.
3. *Kura storehouse* — white plaster block, black tiled roof, black-edged small windows.
4. *Shrine honden* — raised cypress-bark or copper roof with crossed **chigi** and log **katsuogi**
   on the ridge, vermilion posts.
5. *Tea house* — tiny, low, thatch-and-shingle, round window, stepping stones.
6. *Onsen bathhouse* — wide roof with a steam vent cupola.
7. *Arched vermilion bridge* (taikobashi) — half-moon arch reflected into a full circle.

**(b) Hero landmark** — **Five-tier pagoda**: stacked dark-tile roofs with upcurved corners
shrinking upward, vermilion posts, white plaster panels, a bronze finial ring-spire (sōrin).

**(c) Man-made props** — stone **tōrō** lanterns (path and garden forms) · red paper **chōchin**
lanterns under eaves · **andon** floor lamps glowing through paper · vermilion **torii** gates
(single or tunnel of many) · bamboo **inuyarai** curved fence along house walls · **tsukubai**
stone basin with bamboo spout · **shishi-odoshi** see-saw bamboo · wooden **ema** plaque racks ·
noren curtains in indigo · rice-straw **shimenawa** ropes with paper zigzags · wooden market stalls
with cloth awnings · rickshaw · koi-streamers on poles · wind chimes · raked-gravel garden.

**(d) Natural artifacts** — **sakura** in full bloom and falling petals · cloud-pruned black pine ·
maple in red · bamboo grove · moss-covered stones · koi pond with stepping stones · a snow-capped
cone volcano on the horizon · misty layered hills.

**(e) Palette** — `#D8452F` torii vermilion · `#2E3440` roof charcoal · `#F4EFE4` plaster white ·
`#F7B7C8` sakura pink · `#6F8F4E` pine moss

- [Stylized Japanese Market (Sketchfab)](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/stylized-japanese-market-game-ready-env-a66e18a8e79c4e4bb553b00d21ebcb6f)

## 3. Korea

**(a) Signature buildings**
1. *Giwa hanok* — L/U plan around a madang courtyard, white plaster walls framed in timber,
   dark grey **giwa** clay-tile roof with a soft sag and upturned tips.
2. *Choga (thatched hanok)* — rounded rice-straw roof bound with rope nets; humble village house.
3. *Soseul-daemun gate* — tall main gate whose roof rises higher than the walls either side.
4. *Pavilion (jeongja)* — open, raised on posts over water, **dancheong**-painted brackets.
5. *Palace hall* — stacked double roof on a stone plinth, full dancheong in green/red.
6. *Stone wall lane* (Bukchon style) — tile-capped walls stepping up a hill.
7. *Hanji paper shop* — lattice doors (changhoji) glowing warm at night.

**(b) Hero landmark** — **Palace gate with stacked roofs** (Gwanghwamun/Sungnyemun type): a
granite platform with three arches, a two-tier timber roof above, dancheong eaves.

**(c) Man-made props** — **onggi** fermenting jars clustered on the **jangdokdae** terrace (the
single best "reads as Korea" prop) · paper lotus and silk lanterns strung overhead · **faceless**
wooden guardian posts (jangseung silhouette without the carved faces) · **sotdae** bird poles ·
stone stacking cairns · wooden sliding lattice doors · pyeongsang (low wooden platform bed) ·
straw mats · kimchi baskets · stone pagodas (small, three-tier) · hanji lanterns · wooden A-frame
carrier (jige) · well with a small tile roof.

**(d) Natural artifacts** — red pine on granite outcrops · maple canopies in autumn orange ·
persimmon tree with orange fruit · magpies · layered blue ridgelines · plum blossom · reed marsh.

**(e) Palette** (drawn from **obangsaek** + dancheong) — `#2F7D6D` dancheong green ·
`#C8372D` dancheong red · `#4A4F57` giwa grey · `#F2EEE3` plaster white · `#F2B632` lantern gold

## 6. Switzerland

   carved balconies with **red geranium** boxes.
2. *Engadin stone house* — thick white-plastered walls with **sgraffito** grey patterns, deep
   funnel windows, an arched barn door (sulèr).
3. *Appenzell painted house* — gable-front, many small windows in rows, painted pastel facade.
4. *Speicher / raccard granary* — small timber hut on **mushroom stone** legs (anti-mouse discs).
5. *Covered wooden bridge* — timber roofed bridge with flower boxes (Kapellbrücke type).
6. *Mountain hut* — stone ground floor, shingled roof weighted with stones.
7. *Cogwheel railway station* — small red-roof halt.

**(b) Hero landmark** — **Clock tower** (Zytglogge type): square stone tower, a big painted clock
face with gold numerals, astronomical dial, a small spire and bell.

**(c) Man-made props** — stone village **fountain** with a flag-pole column and flower tubs ·

gable ornament · painted shutters (red/white chevrons) · lanterns on iron brackets · wooden bench
