## Required selection loop once access works

Search specific topic → inspect a promising image → record actual pin URL and original creator/source → follow two relevant related-image branches → compare alternatives → retain or reject with a concrete reason. Then change vocabulary using what was observed. No automated bulk harvesting or blind clicking through arrays of guessed URLs.

For every selected image record: region/period, observed shape/material/palette, intended asset/function, modesty or anatomy adaptation, originality/licence status, reason selected, reasons rejected, next two keywords, and primary context source. Pinterest ranking is not cultural evidence.

Minimum production evidence per regional family: streetscape, ordinary home, grocery/workshop, public/library space, countryside/camp where applicable, two daily/work outfits and one occasion outfit. This is a research gate, not permission to claim every region has been reviewed.
