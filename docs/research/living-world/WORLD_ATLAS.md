# Living-world research atlas

2026-09-25. Research and design proposal, not a claim that these places are already built.
Companion files: REQUIREMENTS_EXPANSION.md, LIVING_SYSTEMS.md, BLENDER_WEB_PIPELINE.md, PINTEREST_RESEARCH.md.

## Design finding

Build places from terrain → water → routes → settlement → livelihoods → institutions → objects → stories.
Architecture is evidence of climate, resources, craft, work and household structure. A palette and famous monument alone do not make a culture. Every regional kit needs contemporary residents and ordinary necessities as well as heritage.

For each region, author: elevation/watershed map; wet/dry/cold season; local building materials; city/town/village/camp relationships; employment and trade graph; community institutions; grocery/repair/shelter access; clothes by climate, occupation and occasion; library holdings; three problems with multiple responses; shared-traveller moments; natural and made-object kit; evidence and uncertainty.

## Style axes beyond fantasy and sci-fi

Keep geography, period, occupation and speculative style as independent tags.

- Everyday vernacular: regional climate materials, repaired fabrics, domestic objects.
- Pastoral/cottage: gardens, work aprons, winding footpaths, mills, woven baskets.
- Maritime: boatyards, storm layers, sailcloth, salt-weathered wood, navigation tools.
- Alpine/subarctic: layered wool, insulation, compact storage, steep roofs, sheltered paths.
- Garden-city / solarpunk proposal: shading, public gardens, repairable solar devices and shared infrastructure; use local building traditions as a foundation.
- Artisan / guild / scholarly: looms, printing, notebooks, aprons, libraries, observatories.
- Art Nouveau / Arts and Crafts: botanical geometry, stained-glass-inspired nonfigurative panels, useful handmade furnishings.
- Art Deco / streamline / mid-century: geometric shopfronts, radios, transit interiors and tailored covered clothes.
- Industrial heritage / clockwork fantasy: visible mechanisms and repair; no requirement for militarism or smoke-covered poverty.
- Retro-futurist / space-age / hard-sci-fi utility: modular equipment, protective layers, readable function, bounded technology.
- Mythic pastoral / celestial fantasy / botanical fantasy: light, gardens and invented materials; preserve practical food, transport and maintenance systems.

## Wardrobe construction grammar

Generate a curated garment record, not a random mixture of cultural signs:
reference place + period + occasion + occupation + climate + silhouette + fabric/process + palette + coverage adaptation.
Example: a Korean-inspired everyday jacket silhouette over a full-length skirt, opaque sleeves and fitted-under-outer head covering. Label it an adaptation, not an authentic historical reconstruction.

Build: base trousers and sleeves; tunic/dress; outer layer; head covering/headwear; shoes; glasses; utility accessories. Weight cloth to a shared rig, test seated and flying poses, and disable inappropriate cape collisions before export. Use garment sockets and skin masks to prevent clipping. The male quiff can be covered by a hat; persistent identity does not require it to pierce the hat.

Primary reference anchors: [V&A kimono](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/kimono), [Met Gujarat patola textile](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/456962), [UNESCO batik](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/batik-masterpiece-of-oral-and-intangible-heritage-of-humanity-00170), [Abu Dhabi Culture Al Sadu](https://abudhabiculture.ae/en/cultural-heritage/intangible/unesco-ich-inscribed-elements/al-sadu).

## Asset kit per settlement

Architecture: structural wall/roof modules, thresholds, windows, balconies, shopfront, workshop, library, grocery, shelter, sanitation/service building, vehicle depot, bridge and water feature.
Domestic: cooking vessels, storage jars, bedding, lighting, wash basin, books, rugs, shelves and repair box.
Work: actual tools, raw materials, partially finished goods, transport containers and measuring devices.
Natural: region-specific tree silhouettes, crops, groundcover, rocks, water edges and seasonal variation.
Readable public space: shaded seating, route signs, public water, deliveries, noticeboard and accessible paths.
Do not fill every town with the same crates and lanterns recoloured. Asset provenance, use and region tags should drive distribution.

## Coverage limitations

This is an initial source-grounded framework, not an ethnographic survey or an approved visual board. Pinterest's logged-out session blocks the requested iterative image selection. Many expansion rows are deliberate research leads. Real layouts, scale drawings, clothing provenance and modern local voices must be collected per region before production; museums and UNESCO heritage alone cannot describe all contemporary life.
