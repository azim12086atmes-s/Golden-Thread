# Each land's science — real-world sources

The owner asked us to "search what every place has". Each land's institute in the game teaches a real
tradition of that region (`src/institutions/catalogue.ts`). This note records, for each one, the
real tradition behind it and a public source that describes it. The sources were checked on
2026-09-29 and are mostly UNESCO listings, museums and government bodies.

Two of the institutes are the game's own inventions: the Meadow's **Wizardry** and the Sky Isles'
**Lightcraft**. They have no real-world source.

## Notes for the game text

- **Nile Crossing:** the Rhoda Island nilometer's reading of 16 cubits was the flood that meant a good year. This would make a good line for the Nile institute's first stage.
- **Madinat an-Nur:** Britannica cautions that the House of Wisdom was a royal library. Much of the translating happened elsewhere, so the game's wording should say "great libraries" rather than claim the library itself did the translating.
- **Kaveri Coast:** the spice agronomy and temple engineering in the game's description have no source above yet. Add one when those stages are written.
